# Sreenarayanaguru Open University (SGOU) BCA Full Guide

**Free SGOU BCA notes, previous-year question papers (PYQs), SLM study materials, important questions, and exam resources for Sreenarayanaguru Open University, Kerala.**

This community guide supports students of the **Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)** programme at **Sreenarayanaguru Open University (SGOU)**. It brings together Semester 1 and Semester 2 resources, Semester 3 SLMs, SLM-based notes, combined question banks, repeated questions, C programming problems, and printable PDFs in one place.

> **Status:** Semester 1 and Semester 2 study materials are ready. Semester 3 SLMs are included. Semester 4 is open for previous question papers.  
> **Maintained by:** [Abdul Vahab A A](https://abdulvahabaa.in)

## Official university links

- **Website:** [sgou.ac.in](https://sgou.ac.in)
- **Student / Admission Portal (ERP):** [erp.sgou.ac.in](https://erp.sgou.ac.in)

## Why this repo exists

SGOU BCA students often juggle SLM textbooks, scattered PYQs, and last-minute revision lists. This repository brings those pieces together in one place:

- SLM-aligned study notes
- Combined previous-year question banks
- High-priority / repeatedly asked topics
- C programming practice from exam papers
- PDF copies for offline reading
- Space for assignments, lab records, and future semesters

Everything here is **unofficial** and community-built. Always cross-check with the latest SGOU syllabus and official Self Learning Material (SLM).
